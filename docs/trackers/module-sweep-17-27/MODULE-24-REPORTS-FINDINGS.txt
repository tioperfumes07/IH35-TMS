RPT-1 · MED-HIGH (compliance) · IFTA due KPI computes the WRONG quarter — under-warns by a full quarter
- Live proof: today 2026-07-02 the KPI reads "IFTA Q3 DUE · 122d · 2026-10-31 — file before". The IFTA return actually due next is Q2 (quarter ended 06/30), due 2026-07-31 — 29 days away.
- Root cause (apps/backend/src/reports/shared.ts getCurrentQuarterInfo): it computes the CURRENT calendar quarter and its future deadline. During the filing month (the month after quarter end), the just-ended quarter's deadline is the one that matters; the function skips it entirely. On 2026-07-30 — one day before the real Q2 deadline — this KPI would say "Q3 due in 93d". For a DOT/IFTA compliance KPI that is a real risk.
- Fix (root): next-due logic — if today ≤ (last day of month following the PREVIOUS quarter end), the due filing is the PREVIOUS quarter with that deadline; otherwise the current quarter with its deadline. Covers the January case (prior-year Q4 due Jan 31). Unit tests at boundaries: Jul 1, Jul 31, Aug 1, Jan 1, Jan 31, Feb 1. Cite the Texas Comptroller rule already referenced in the code comment.
