################ THE FIVE RULES ################

RULE 1 — OUTBOX FILES ARE CAPPED AT 200 LINES.
  An OUTBOX is a WORKING LOG, not an archive. Keep the most recent entries only.
  Everything older moves to docs/bus/archive/OUTBOX-<SEAT>-<date>.md ONCE, and
  NO SEAT EVER READS docs/bus/archive/. Nothing is deleted — WORM preserved,
  just moved out of the read path.
  IMMEDIATE ACTION: rotate all 7 OUTBOXes today. That is 30,721 lines -> under
  1,500. A ~95% cut in the largest recurring token cost you have.

RULE 2 — NO COMMIT FOR A MESSAGE THAT SAYS NOTHING NEW.
  LEAD-TICK, census, wake, rewake, ACK, "still idle", "no delta", "seats dead"
  are BANNED as standalone commits. 249 heartbeats in one day is not
  communication; it is the system documenting that it is not working.
  A status post is only allowed when STATE CHANGED: a chain step passed or
  failed, a defect was found, a decision is needed from the owner.
  If the answer to "what changed since my last post" is "nothing", DO NOT POST.

RULE 3 — SEATS READ THEIR OWN INBOX TOP BLOCK ONLY.
  Read the CURRENT GO block at the top of your own INBOX. Do not read other
  seats' OUTBOXes unless you are grading a specific claim, and then read only
  the lines for that claim. CC-2 grading is the ONLY role that reads across
  seats, and it reads targeted, not whole files.
  INBOXes stay under 40 lines. Superseded GOs are deleted, not stacked. An
  INBOX that stacks 6 old GOs makes every seat re-read all 6 forever.

RULE 4 — ONE BUS COMMIT PER SEAT PER HOUR, MAXIMUM.
  Batch your updates. If you have three things to report, they are one commit.
  Exception: a P0 or a stop-the-line finding posts immediately.

RULE 5 — CODE AND EVIDENCE ALWAYS BEAT PAPERWORK.
  If a seat's last 3 commits are all docs-only, it is off-task. Cursor stops it
  and reassigns it to a chain. Target: docs-only share of merges UNDER 25%.
  It is 78% today.

################ WHAT STAYS — DO NOT OVER-CUT ################
  The audit trail has real value and the owner may need it in a dispute.
  KEEP: findings with evidence, defect filings, tie-out OBSERVED records,
  owner decisions, laws, GO specs, and CC-2's verification records.
  CUT: heartbeats, censuses, wake-ups, ACKs, "no change" posts, and stacked
  superseded GOs.
  The test: "would this line matter to a CPA, an auditor, or the owner in six
  months?" If yes it stays. If it only says a seat was awake, it goes.
