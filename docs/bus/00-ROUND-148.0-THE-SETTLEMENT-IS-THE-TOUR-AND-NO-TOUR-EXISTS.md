> "settlements are tours."

**CLOSED = settlement** (frozen, posts to GL, company and driver readouts).
The round trip is the unit: **NB opens · TR extends (0..n) · SB closes it at Laredo.**
Assignment is **automatic and immediate at booking**. Closing is now **automatic when the tour-closing load
closes** (owner ruling 2026-09-23, superseding the earlier "confirmed by a human" line in
`claude/00-IH35-CURRENT-STATE-AND-LAW-READ-FIRST.md` §2).
A tour has **no unit** — the unit lives on the leg. A tour may have more than one driver; settle each for the
legs they drove, all linked to one company settlement.
