# THE $500.01 ESCROW RESIDUAL — EXPLAINED, WITH THE ROWS

**Lead, 2026-09-23. Measured live on production `br-fancy-credit-akjnd07a`, `SET LOCAL
app.bypass_rls = 'lucia'`. Nothing was written.**

CC-3 reported that after mirroring all 61 real escrow postings, 3 of 42
`accounting.escrow_accounts` rows stayed non-zero by **$500.01 total**, "not explained by
postings in scope." He was right to refuse to close E10 on it. Here is what it is.

## It is three repair pairs from 2026-09-02 where the release is exactly DOUBLE the deposit

| escrow account | deposit | release | net |
|---|---|---|---|
| `b46f3e8a-10b5-4716-971e-08d9db383c0b` | $0.01 | $0.02 | **−$0.01** |
| `1a450978-135f-4c0f-ba5c-8e161e745891` | $250.00 | $500.00 | **−$250.00** |
| `93358b4d-f4ba-465e-bcdb-d528186c45ae` | $250.00 | $500.00 | **−$250.00** |
| | | | **−$500.01** |

All six rows carry `source_type = 'reconciliation'`, `source_id = NULL`, no linked journal
entry, and notes that name themselves:

- `AUDIT-TRAIL-GAP MARK (1/2) — restates the $250.00 balance this escrow account…`
- `WORM REVERSE (2/2) — zeroes this account per owner ruling docs/lockdo…`

## The arithmetic, exactly

    sum of all escrow_accounts.balance_cents (10 non-zero) ......  132,500
    signed sum of all escrow_postings (222,501 dep − 140,002 rel)   82,499
    difference .................................................    50,001  =  $500.01

## What actually happened

On 2026-09-02 a two-step repair ran: **MARK** restated a balance the account *already held*, then
**WORM REVERSE** zeroed the account. But the release was written for the restated amount **plus**
the original — double the deposit. The account ends correctly at **0**, which is why nobody saw
it. The *posting ledger* is what ends $500.01 short of the account balances.

## The finding, plainly

- **No money is missing.** All three accounts read `balance_cents = 0` right now. Verified live.
- **This is not a defect in CC-3's mirror.** He mirrored a ledger that was already internally
  out of agreement by exactly $500.01, three weeks before tonight's work.
- **It is an audit-trail artifact**, not a balance: a difference between two representations of
  the same zero.

## The ruling — and why the obvious fix is wrong

The instinct is to append a correcting deposit of $0.01 + $250.00 + $250.00 to bring the ledger
into agreement. **That is wrong and must not be done.** `trg_apply_escrow_posting_delta` applies
every insert to the account balance, so three correcting deposits would move three accounts that
are correctly at zero **off** zero. The cure would create the disease.

**RULING: leave the six rows alone. E10's mirror must SKIP them.**

A correction pair that already nets its account to the intended state does not get mirrored —
mirroring it produces a correction of a correction, which is exactly the $500.01 CC-3 was
staring at. The mirror's rule is: **mirror postings that moved a balance to its current value;
skip postings that were themselves a repair pair.** These six identify themselves in their own
notes and by `source_id IS NULL` with no linked journal entry.

**E10 is unblocked. The residual is explained, it is zero dollars of real money, and the
accounts it touches are already at zero.**
