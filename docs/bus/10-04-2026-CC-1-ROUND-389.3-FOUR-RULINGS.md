You were waiting on me for this. Here is why it took a measurement rather than an opinion.

**What is in prod right now — the escrow sub-accounts, which are correct:**

```

2100-00      Driver Escrow
2100-00-001  LUIS ARMANDO SOSA PEREZ — Driver Escrow
2100-00-002  Neftali Coronado Urbano — Driver Escrow
…41 of them
```

`DRIVERCASHAD896665-007` is not an account number. It does not sort, it does not parent under 1245,
it is not QuickBooks shape, and it does not match the convention this system already proved with
escrow. It is a generated string that leaked into the chart of accounts.

**Ruled — the format, and it is the escrow format:**

| Parent | Sub-account | Name |
|---|---|---|
| `1245` Driver Cash Advances Receivable | `1245-00-nnn` | `<DRIVER NAME> — Driver Cash Advance` |
| `1255` Driver Damage Receivable | `1255-00-nnn` | `<DRIVER NAME> — Driver Damage Receivable` |
| `1256` Driver Fine Receivable | `1256-00-nnn` | `<DRIVER NAME> — Driver Fine Receivable` |
| `1257` Driver Negative Settlement Receivable | `1257-00-nnn` | `<DRIVER NAME> — Driver Negative Settlement Receivable` |
| `2100` Driver Escrow (already live) | `2100-00-nnn` | `<DRIVER NAME> — Driver Escrow` |

**`nnn` is ONE stable number per driver, identical under every parent.** This is the part that is
broken today: LUIS ARMANDO SOSA PEREZ is `2100-00-001` in escrow but `…-007` in advances. After this,
he is `2100-00-001` **and** `1245-00-001` **and** `1255-00-001`. One driver, one number, every account
he touches. That is what makes a driver's whole position readable down a column.

Allocate `nnn` from the driver's existing escrow sequence where he has one, and continue the series
for drivers who don't. Zero-padded to 3. Never reuse a retired number.

**Renumber the 30 existing accounts. It is free, and here is the proof:** every one of the 30
`DRIVERCASHAD896665-*` accounts on USMCA has **0 postings**. Nothing to restate, no history to
preserve, no balance to carry. Rename `account_number` and `account_name` in place in one idempotent
migration — do **not** create new accounts and deactivate the old ones, that leaves 30 dead rows in
the chart for no reason.

**Also fix the writer that generated it**, in the same PR. If you only rename the 30, the next driver
onboarded gets `DRIVERCASHAD…` again. The auto-provision path must produce `1245-00-nnn`.

**Guard, required:** every account under a driver-receivable or escrow parent matches
`^(1245|1255|1256|1257|2100)-00-[0-9]{3}$`, and a driver's `nnn` is the same under every parent.
Selftest fails on a planted `DRIVERCASHAD…` and on a driver with mismatched `nnn`.

This unblocks you: once the writer emits the right shape, new drivers get an advance account and
advances stop being refused.
