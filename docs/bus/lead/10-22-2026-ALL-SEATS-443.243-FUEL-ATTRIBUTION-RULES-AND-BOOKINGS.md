## THE RULE (permanent, one engine: resolveFillTruckAndLoad — replace the 443.214 "nearest GPS truck" step)
All times America/Chicago.
1. SETTLEMENT FIRST: a fill printed on a signed settlement (driver or company side) belongs to that settlement's load, on the settlement's date. Done — certain.
2. CARD TRUCK KEEPS ITS FUEL: otherwise the fill belongs to the CARD's truck. Its load = the card truck's load whose trip window contains the fill moment. Trip window = first EMPTY/PICKUP stop → LAST EMPTY stop on the signed settlement (return to yard) — not the delivery (443.241 extract gives the legs).
3. NO LOAD AT THAT MOMENT → the card truck's NEXT load (first pickup after the fill), any number of days later; never a load completed before the fill; if not booked yet → "pending next load", attaches automatically when that truck's next load is booked.
4. EVIDENCE that confirms or contradicts (record each on the fill): card truck's Samsara GPS position at the fill moment vs the station (distance, minutes), the Samsara driver logged into that truck (a driver can have several Samsara users/profiles — match all of them), odometer at the fill vs the load's odometer window, settlement / AlwaysTrack mileage reports for that truck.
5. 100% CERTAIN = rule 1, OR rule 2/3 with the card truck's GPS at the station (≤0.3 mi, ±20 min) and no contradicting evidence. ANYTHING ELSE (card truck GPS elsewhere, another of our trucks at the pump, no time of day, no GPS, odometer outside the window, driver on a different truck) = NOT certain → the engine does NOT assign by itself; it creates a FUEL ASSIGNMENT QUESTION with the evidence and a proposed answer.
6. Every fill always ends on a load (owner: "we always assign to a load"). No fill is ever moved off the card truck without the owner/dispatcher confirming it in the question.
