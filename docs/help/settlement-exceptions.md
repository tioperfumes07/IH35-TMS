# Settlement exceptions and disputes

Exceptions are settlement lines or periods that cannot auto-close — missing POD, rate disputes, deduction challenges, or data mismatches.

## Overview
- Office users review exception queues; drivers may submit disputes through the allowed driver/office dispute path when that workflow is enabled.
- Dispute records are permanent history: decisions and reasons stay auditable.

## Key tasks
- Triage exceptions by pay period and driver.
- Attach evidence (POD, rate confirmation, fine notice) via Documents where required.
- Decide: adjust the settlement, reject the dispute with reason, or hold for Owner.
- Re-run settlement totals after adjustments before approval.

## Tips & gotchas
- Do not “fix” an exception by deleting lines — adjust with reason and audit.
- Safety fines and escrow deductions should link to their source records so reverse drill-through works.
- If two dispute ledgers still appear in older docs, follow the current Settlements UI path for your environment and escalate dual-path bugs rather than writing to both.
