# Reports hub navigation

The Reports hub is the catalog of operational and financial reports available for the current company and your role.

## Overview
- Open **Reports** from the left rail.
- Reports are grouped (ops, finance, compliance, etc.). Each report opens its own filter bar and export actions.
- Many reports respect the operating-company switcher and date range filters.

## Key tasks
- Find a report by name or group, then set date range and entity filters.
- Export CSV/PDF when the report offers it for auditors or lenders.
- Drill into linked records (load, driver, invoice) from report rows when EntityLinks are present.
- Prefer scheduled delivery for recurring packs (see *Scheduled reports*).

## Tips & gotchas
- A zero report is not always a bug — confirm company, dates, and status filters.
- Financial reports follow TMS books; they will not auto-match QBO until reconcile processes are run.
- Role gating may hide Owner-only packs from dispatchers.
