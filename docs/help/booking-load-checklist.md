# Booking a load — checklist

Use this checklist before you mark a load ready for the road.

## Before booking
1. Correct **operating company** selected.
2. **Customer** exists with billing/credit data; credit limit source understood (factor / manual / rmis_future).
3. **Origin/destination**, commodity, equipment type, and dates entered.
4. Rate and accessorials match the customer agreement.
5. Any special instructions (border, appointments, temperature) recorded on the load.

## Assignment
1. Pick a **driver** who is active and not blocked (HOS violation hard-block, DVIR major defect, etc.).
2. Pick a **unit** (and trailer if required) that is not OOS / overdue in a way that blocks dispatch.
3. Confirm factoring packet / customer factor routing if the customer is factored.
4. Save and verify the load appears on the board with the expected status.

## After booking
- Watch detention and appointment times.
- Capture POD/BOL through Driver App or office Documents as your SOP requires.
- Hand off to settlements/factoring when the trip completes — do not leave revenue orphaned from the load.
