THE OWNER'S INSTRUCTION, AND WHY IT IS THE RIGHT TEST
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"upload all invoices, settlements, factoring purchases one by one in the app by the
coders, as if they were human creating and following the transactions, making sure that
everything is recording as it should be. this should show us for these 6 modules at least
if they are truly done."

That is a better test than any checklist, and it replaces guesswork with a business
process. A module is done when a person can carry a real transaction through it and the
money lands correctly at every step. Anything that cannot be done in the UI, or that
records the wrong number, is the answer.

RULES THAT MAKE THIS A TEST INSTEAD OF A DATA LOAD:
 1. GO THROUGH THE APP. No bulk SQL insert. No seed script. If a step cannot be done in
    the UI, THAT IS THE FINDING - write it down and keep going.
 2. ONE AT A TIME, IN DATE ORDER. Real sequence, real dependencies.
 3. DO NOT FIX FORWARD SILENTLY. If a screen forces a wrong value, record what it forced
    and what the right value was. Do not hand-correct in the database afterwards - that
    erases the evidence the owner is paying for.
 4. RECORD EVERY BLOCKER with: screen, field, what you expected, what happened.
 5. USMCA ONLY. TRANSP and TRK stay frozen. Their loads are a report, not a build.
