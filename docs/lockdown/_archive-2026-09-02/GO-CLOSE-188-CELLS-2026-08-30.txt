  C25 GL D  (gl_delta)
    Every money-moving action on this leaf produces a balanced journal entry
    whose debits equal credits, and the GL delta equals the amount shown on the
    surface. Proof: create through the UI, read accounting.journal_entry_postings,
    assert sum(debit) = sum(credit) and delta = displayed amount.

  C26 SUB  (subledger_tie)
    The module's own subledger total ties to its GL control account for a stated
    period. AR subledger = AR control; driver escrow subledger = escrow liability;
    AP subledger = AP control. Proof: a query returning subledger sum minus GL
    control sum, asserting zero. This is the classic GL-to-subledger tie.

  C27 LIFE  (lifecycle_complete)
    Every status the record can hold is reachable AND every terminal status
    posts or reverses correctly - no status that strands money. Proof: a table
    of statuses with the GL effect of each transition, all exercised.

  C28 REV  (reversal_symmetry)
    Voiding or reversing a posted transaction writes an equal and opposite
    entry - it never deletes or edits the original. Debits and credits swap;
    the original stays readable. Proof: post, reverse, assert two entries exist
    and net to zero. QuickBooks works this way and so must we.

  C29 PER  (period_guard)
    No transaction may post into a closed accounting period. Reopening is an
    owner-only action that is recorded. NetSuite states a closed period
    "prevents posting to the general ledger for any dates included in the
    period, by anyone," with system notes retaining an audit trail.
    *** THIS IS UNBUILDABLE TODAY - THERE IS NO PERIOD CLOSE IN THE BACKEND. ***
    16 of the 112 cells are C29. They stay red until Cursor ships period close.

  C30 ENT  (entity_isolation)
    A user scoped to TRANSP can never read or write a USMCA row, at the database
    layer, not the UI layer. Proof: same query with each company context, RLS on,
    asserting zero cross-entity rows.

  C31 LIVE (non_empty_proof)
    The surface renders real rows from prod on the current deploy - not an empty
    state, not a fixture. Proof: authenticated live read on the live healthz SHA
    returning count > 0.
