IH35-TMS · Design proposal

Enter every cost from inside the load itself

One screen where you open a load, see its real state, and add every diesel, toll, lumper and vendor bill against it — each one linked to the load the moment it is created, numbered automatically, and already a candidate for the bank match.

Where it goes

A thirteenth tab, not a fourteenth screen

The load detail already has twelve tabs: Overview, Stops, Driver Pay, Documents, Factoring, Customs, Cargo Sensors, Settlement, Geofence Timeline, Assignment History, Audit, Pre-Settlement. There is a place for what you pay the driver and a place for the settlement — and nowhere at all for what the company spends on the load. That is the hole.

So this becomes tab thirteen, Costs, on the load detail you already have. Not a new page. The app is already carrying 575 screens and 509 of them cannot even be deep-linked; a parallel accounting-side load page would be a second way to do the same thing, and second ways are how two numbers start disagreeing.

Door 1 — Dispatch
Open a load on the board, click Costs. Same drawer you use today, one more tab.
Door 2 — Accounting
A list of live loads with what has been spent on each so far. Click one, land on the same Costs tab. One screen, two ways in.

Why it is worth building

Four problems this closes at once

  • The link cannot be forgotten. A cost created here is born attached to the load. Today the link is a field somebody has to remember to fill, and the ones nobody fills are the ones that never reach per-load margin.
  • The numbering happens by itself. First cost is 3232, then 3232-1, 3232-2. You never type a number and two people entering costs on the same load cannot collide.
  • The bank match gets a short list. Matching against 11,651 transactions is guesswork. Matching a $612.40 Love's charge against the three costs already sitting on load 3232 is not.
  • Margin is visible while the load is still moving. Revenue, costs, driver pay and margin on one screen, before the settlement closes — not discovered a week later.
Rules it must follow

The parts that are easy to get wrong

Expense or Bill is a real accounting choice, not a preference

Paid now is an Expense and credits the bank or card. Owed is a Bill and credits Accounts Payable, then clears later with a bill payment. The toggle sits on every row because a single load routinely has both. Never default it silently — an unpaid bill recorded as a paid expense understates what the company owes.

Nothing saves until it has an account and a payment source

The system already refuses a cost with no payment account and no vendor. Keep that here. A row that cannot post should say so on the row, in English, not fail quietly on Save.

One screen, one write path

This tab must call the same expense and bill creation endpoints everything else uses. If it gets its own path, two screens will start disagreeing about what a cost is, and that is exactly the class of defect this whole build has been digging out.
