# Quality hardline (owner session law)

We never take the short or easy way if it creates risk, weak architecture, confusion, future bugs, financial mistakes, or unfinished work. We do not patch over problems. We do not defer important issues just because they are complicated. We do not guess. We fix the root cause correctly.

Goal: trustworthy software matching/surpassing QuickBooks, NetSuite, McLeod, Alvys, and serious TMS/ERP worldwide.

## Conflicts → choose
Speed vs trust → **Trust** · Easy vs correct → **Correct** · Guess vs verify → **Verify** · Move forward vs protect company → **Protect company**

## Required
Investigate before recommending. No temporary patches. No “done” without proof. No financial/QBO/RLS/migration/security decisions without proof. Research QB/NetSuite/McLeod/Alvys for material recommendations. Court/CPA/auditor-grade honesty.
