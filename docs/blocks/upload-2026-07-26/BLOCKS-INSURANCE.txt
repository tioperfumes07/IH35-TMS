  arbitrary account. Reuse the poster — no new GL math.

  insurance-payable accounts; NEVER an ORDER-BY-created_at pick.

GUARD: scripts/verify-fleet-premium-role-resolved.mjs — FAIL if the account picker uses created_at/LIMIT;
